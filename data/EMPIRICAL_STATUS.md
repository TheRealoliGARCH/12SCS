# Empirical 12SCCM Matrix Status

## Current repository state

The repository contains dimension-specific datasets for all twelve capability dimensions:

$$
N,M,E,F,T,I,R,H,L,D,A,S.
$$

The master recognition ledger contains the nuclear-armed-State observations together with completed non-nuclear strategic-deterrence observations for Switzerland, Belgium and Taiwan. Dimension-specific datasets remain authoritative for empirical matrix assembly.

## State-vector completeness

The non-nuclear strategic-deterrence protocol has now been applied and evidence-ledger records have been added for the three previously pending States:

- **Switzerland:** Level 2, based on armed neutrality, territorial defence, resilience and structured security cooperation.
- **Belgium:** Level 2, based principally on NATO collective defence and alliance-enabled deterrence, without treating alliance deterrence as an independent Belgian nuclear arsenal.
- **Taiwan:** Level 3, based on resilient overall defence, asymmetric and multi-domain denial, whole-of-society resilience and security cooperation, without inferring nuclear capability.

Therefore the twelve-State system now has an explicit observation for every State--capability pair:

$$
\boxed{12\times12=144\text{ observed State--capability entries at the recognition layer}.}
$$

The distinction remains essential:

$$
\text{non-nuclear strategic deterrence}\neq\text{nuclear possession}.
$$

## Anti-circularity rule

The convergence analysis must not be used to revise the underlying recognition scores. Measurement precedes convergence analysis; convergence analysis is downstream of the evidence ledger.
